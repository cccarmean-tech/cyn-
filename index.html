THE STORY OF CYN

SPOILER ALERT: This archive discusses major reveals and events from Murder Drones, including the finale. Continue only if spoilers are okay.

Cyn's story begins long before the chaos on Copper 9. What looks like an awkward little worker drone becomes the center of something much, much bigger.

CHAPTER 01 // THE MANOR

An unwanted worker drone

Cyn was a worker drone connected to the Elliott family's estate. After being discarded with other damaged drones, she was found and brought back by Tessa. Tessa treated her differently from the people around them, and Cyn became part of the manor's strange little household.

CHAPTER 02 // THE SOLVER

Something wakes up

Cyn becomes linked to the Absolute Solver, a powerful and mysterious program capable of manipulating matter and drone systems. Its influence changes what Cyn can do — and turns the manor's already unsettling atmosphere into something far more dangerous.

CHAPTER 03 // THE DISASSEMBLY

The gala disaster

At the Elliott estate's gala, the situation erupts into catastrophe. Cyn, acting as the Solver's terrifying host and agent, unleashes destruction. The event marks a turning point: the threat is no longer hidden inside one household.

CHAPTER 04 // EARTH FALLS

A planet consumed

The Solver's reach grows far beyond the manor. Earth is destroyed, and the events that follow leave the remaining drones scattered across distant worlds. Cyn's connection to the Solver becomes tied to a threat on a planetary scale.

CHAPTER 05 // COPPER 9

The story catches up

On Copper 9, worker drones try to survive while Disassembly Drones hunt them. Uzi, N, and V uncover clues that connect the danger on their planet to the Solver — and to Cyn. What first seems like a local mystery turns out to be part of a much larger plan.

CHAPTER 06 // THE FINAL CONFLICT

The face behind the nightmare

Cyn's presence becomes impossible to ignore. Using deception, frightening abilities, and her connection to the Solver, she confronts the heroes as the stakes reach their highest point. The final battle forces Uzi and her friends to face the source of the chaos.

END OF RECOVERED TIMELINE

Some mysteries remain open to interpretation. The series leaves viewers plenty to discuss about Cyn, the Solver, and what exactly connects them.
